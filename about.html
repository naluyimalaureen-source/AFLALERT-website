About AflAlert

AflAlert App is an AI-powered mobile application designed to help farmers and maize traders detect possible aflatoxin contamination quickly and conveniently. The app uses artificial intelligence and smartphone technology to analyse maize images and provide fast risk assessment without requiring expensive laboratory equipment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make food safety technology accessible to farmers and traders by providing an affordable AI-based aflatoxin detection solution that helps protect communities and improve maize quality.
