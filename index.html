About AflAlert

AflAlert System is an AI-powered mobile application designed to help farmers and maize traders detect possible aflatoxin contamination quickly and conveniently. The system uses artificial intelligence and smartphone technology to analyse maize images and provide fast risk assessment without requiring expensive laboratory equipment.

Why Choose AflAlert?

Offline Detection

The AI model can analyse maize samples without depending on continuous internet connectivity.

Smartphone Scanning

Users can capture maize images using a smartphone camera and receive instant analysis.

Luganda Support

The application supports local users by improving accessibility for farmers and communities.
