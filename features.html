App Features

Offline Detection

The AI model can analyse maize samples without depending on continuous internet connectivity.

Smartphone Scanning

Users can capture maize images using a smartphone camera and receive instant analysis.

Luganda Support

The full app interface is available in Luganda as well as English, improving accessibility for local farmers and communities.

Lateral Strip Scan

Photograph a chemical test strip and get an estimated aflatoxin reading alongside image-based detection — a quick screening aid, not a substitute for certified lab testing.

AI Voice Assistant

Get hands-free guidance and spoken results while scanning your maize.

Secure Scan History

Your scans are safely stored and synced with your account, so you can review results anytime.
