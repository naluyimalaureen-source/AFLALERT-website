App Features

Offline Detection

The AI model can analyse maize samples without depending on continuous internet connectivity.

Smartphone Scanning

Users can capture maize images using a smartphone camera and receive instant analysis.

weather forecasting

The application can fetch and forecast weather basng on the user's current location .

Alert Notifications

the appplication can timely alert and notify farmers on how to prepare for optimal harvesting.

Luganda Support

The application supports local users by improving accessibility for farmers and communities.

Lateral Strip Scan

Scan chemical test strips for precise ppb-level aflatoxin readings, giving lab-grade accuracy alongside image-based detection.

AI Voice Assistant

Get hands-free guidance and spoken results while scanning your maize.

Secure Scan History

Your scans are safely stored and synced with your account, so you can review results anytime.
